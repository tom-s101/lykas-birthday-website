Happy First Monthsarry

from Matheson, to his Dearest Lyka

My Dearest Lyka, the one who is the most precious and important person to me in this world!

Happy First Monthsarry!!!

I'm not quite sure where to start, the words don't seem to come in the way I want them to, and this is the fourth time I'm starting over again, since August 5! (I've tried multiple times to write this, but I just couldn't seem to find the words).

My words in writing may fail to describe and tell of how amazing you are and how thankfully I am for YOU. I have tried my best to put down my thoughts and feeling, but it still doesn't seem to express it fully. But here it goes, as I want to tell you, as it's truly from my heart.

I always wondered how would I know when I have found the woman, the one I would marry? How would I know that this is the woman that God chose for me? How would I know that this is the woman that would love me with her whole heart? That the love she professed was not just words, but that she really meant it? That she would not just leave me if someone else came along.

I just wanted someone who would always be there for me. Someone to stand by my side. Someone to share and experience life with. Someone to grow old together with. Someone that I could share the little moments of the day with. Someone who would help me deepen my relationship with God. Someone to be my lifelong companion and best friend. But most importantly, someone that would be with me to serve Him, wherever He calls.

These are the things I thought a lot about and prayed for continuously…

And then….

I met YOU!!

That's when I knew my prayers were answered!

I am still blown away that you are my girlfriend!!! I have the most wonderful, loving, kind, caring, compassionate, generous girlfriend! The one who is truly mine! I could go on, and on and on about YOU!!! But it's amazing how God brought you to my life, He seemed to know exactly the type of person I wanted and who I needed and that was YOU!! In everything, from what I dreamed of in a woman, the type of person I preferred, even the unprayed for qualities in someone, (because I didn't want to limit God). You have them all and way more besides! More than I ever asked or prayed for!

I never would've thought that we would celebrate our first month together, from that first seemingly chance meeting at PYC last June. But yet, here we are!!! I remember in the months after we first met, of dreaming and hoping that maybe you would somehow, in a very tiny and minuscule chance, come to like me, but that idea seemed so utterly far and impossible that I had to check myself, because in reality, for me, there was no way in the whole world that would be possible. It was probably more likely for me to win the lottery, or get struck by lightning, or land on the moon. But now, what I thought would only be fairytale fiction is now really true! YOU ARE MY GIRLFRIEND!!!! (I'm smiling so much just writing this out xD)

Words still fail to express the deep gratitude that I have for you, and my thankfulness to God for bringing you into my life. Looking back, this was the perfect timing! Every day, I still thank God for YOU!!!! Because of how He answered all my prayers in the most remarkable and clear manner! Really making it crystal clear, that He is the one who led YOU to me, and will continue to guide our relationship! With the providential answers to prayers with YOU, I have never doubted that you were the one for me. Now, hearing your side, and what you shared with how you've been praying also, and how He answered your questions and prayers in a very clear way, makes me even more amazed and happy that God wasn't just leading me to you, but that He was also leading you to me!!! Knowing it was not just my prayers being answered but yours also… leaves me just speechless!!! He is a really good, kind, and loving God!!!

I know our relationship won't always be easy and without issues in the future, but we need to remember how God has led us together, and not let the enemy divide us! Whatever may happen, I will always be by your side, loving you the best I know (and I want to learn how to love you even better), being with you through the easy and the tough times, and supporting you in the decisions that you make! Forever!!! ♾️ Until He returns to take us to a better place!!! As we continue our life journey together I look forward to how God will continue to guide! I KNOW He has led us this far, and I want Him to continue to lead forever. Because when we are in the center of His will, that's where we are the happiest and safest!

Forever and for eternity, I want to always be with YOU!!! I LOVE YOU, my Dearest Lyka!!!

For our first monthsarry of countless millions more (we can still celebrate them in heaven ;>)

The Boyfriend who won't ever let YOU out of his mind or heart! — Matheson
